We arrived late on Sunday and walked straight down to the river. The city is built on
hills, so every street ends in a view.

![The harbour at dawn, with a boat on the water and the sun low over the hills](harbour.svg)

## The old town

The next morning we took the **tram 28** up to Alfama. It is slow, loud and *always* full,
and it is still the best way to see the old town. The route is on the
[city transport site](https://www.carris.pt "Carris, the Lisbon transport company").

## What to eat

A `pastel de nata` costs about a euro and is best eaten warm, standing at the counter.
We had one every morning.

### One practical note

Wear shoes with a grip. The pavements are polished stone, and after rain they are as
smooth as glass.
